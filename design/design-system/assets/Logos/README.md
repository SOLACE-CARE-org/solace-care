# Logos

The Solace Care marks, copied from the screens as supplied. Use them as they are: never redraw, recolour, stretch or add effects.

- `solace-care-wordmark.svg` — the wordmark. Welcome and guest-join screens, at 108×36 (96×32 on guest join), on `surface-page`, and in the admin sidebar at 96×32 on `surface-card`, beside the "Admin" pill. Its `alt` is "Solace Care".
- `solace-care-splash.svg` — the splash lockup, 200×205, centred on `brand-splash` with two `brand-splash-ring` discs behind it. Decorative there: the link around it is labelled "Solace Care. Tap to continue". The admin sign-in panel uses it at 104×107 on the same `brand-splash` ground with the same discs.
- `solace-care-mark.svg` — the small mark above the language choice, 54×44, on `surface-page`. Decorative (`alt=""`).
- `solace-care-app-icon.svg` — the app icon as it shows in notification previews, 32×32 with `radius-8` corners. Also the source for store icons.

Clear space: at least half the mark's height on every side. Don't place the wordmark on `brand` or photos; on dark grounds use the splash lockup.

## Getting the files

The four SVGs are not in this bundle yet. Download them from the Logos group of the Solace Care design system (https://claude.ai/artifact/M78BFurzPEz2mJPDAn3g5H) and save them here under the names above. Until then, the screens show a placeholder where each logo goes. Do not draw a substitute logo.
